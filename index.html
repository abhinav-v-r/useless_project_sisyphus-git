The Philosophy

ABOUT

Embrace the Absurd

The Global Leaderboard of Despair is a monument to the endless, repetitive nature of software development. Like Sisyphus condemned to push a boulder up a mountain only to watch it roll back down, we too commit code that inevitably requires more fixes, more refactoring, more debugging.

When you commit code, you aren't just saving filesΓÇöyou're raging against the void. This leaderboard tracks the misery of developers worldwide. The greater your despair, the higher you climb.

"One must imagine Sisyphus happy." But looking at this code base, we are definitely not.

The Parameters

RULES

How to Play the Game You Can't Win

1. Commit Your Misery: Every time you try to commit, the Sisyphus AI Philosopher will interrogate the purpose of your existence and the futility of your code.

2. Accept Defeat: To proceed, you must type a message acknowledging the ultimate meaninglessness of your task. A high 'despair score' allows the commit to pass.

3. Climb the Ranks: Your accumulated despair scores and total commits dictate your rank on this leaderboard. Will you be the king of the cursed?
